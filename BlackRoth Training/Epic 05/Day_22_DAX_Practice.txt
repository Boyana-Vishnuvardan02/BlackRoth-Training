DAY 22 - DAX PRACTICE

Model:
Sales = Fact table
Customers = Dimension table
Products = Dimension table
Date = Dimension table

Relationships:
Date[Date] 1 -> * Sales[Order_Date]
Customers[Customer_ID] 1 -> * Sales[Customer_ID]
Products[Product_ID] 1 -> * Sales[Product_ID]

Basic Measures:
Total Sales = SUM(Sales[Sales_Amount])
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[Order_ID])
Total Quantity = SUM(Sales[Quantity])
Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders])
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[Customer_ID])

Optional calculated column:
Line Total = Sales[Quantity] * Sales[Unit_Price]

Calculated Column vs Measure:
- Calculated column is evaluated row by row and stored in the model.
- Measure is calculated when a visual/query is evaluated and responds to filters/slicers.
