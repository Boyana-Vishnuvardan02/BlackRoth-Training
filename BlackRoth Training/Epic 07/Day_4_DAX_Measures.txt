DAY 4 — DAX MEASURES
Created each as a separate measure: Modeling > New measure.

Total Revenue = SUM(Sales[Revenue])
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[Order_ID])
Total Units = SUM(Sales[Quantity])
AOV = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Orders])
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[Customer_ID])
Total Cost = SUM(Sales[Cost])
Total Profit = [Total Revenue] - [Total Cost]
Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue])
Revenue PY = CALCULATE([Total Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Date'[Date]))
Revenue YoY % = DIVIDE([Total Revenue] - [Revenue PY], [Revenue PY])
Revenue YTD = TOTALYTD([Total Revenue], 'Date'[Date])
Orders per Customer = DIVIDE([Total Orders], [Total Customers])

Formatting:
- Revenue, AOV, Cost, Profit, Revenue PY, Revenue YTD: Currency (₹)
- Orders, Units, Customers: Whole number
- Profit Margin %, Revenue YoY %: Percentage with 1 decimal
